Classic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until the ingredients are combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan and cook both sides until golden.
  6. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.

Fresh Fruit Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 apple
  • 1 banana
  • 1 orange
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • 1 tablespoon honey

Instructions

  1. Wash and cut the fruits into small pieces.
  2. Place all the fruits in a large bowl.
  3. Add honey and gently mix.
  4. Serve immediately or chill before serving.
